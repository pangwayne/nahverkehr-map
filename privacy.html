2. Visiting this app

When you open the app, your browser sends technical data to my hosting provider Cloudflare: your IP address, date and time, the address requested (for example which stop's departures), the page you came from, and your browser and operating system. Cloudflare uses this data to deliver the app and to protect it from attacks. The photo in the header comes from my website www.pangwayne.com, which Cloudflare also hosts, so the same applies there. Cloudflare also keeps short-lived request logs that help me find errors. If Cloudflare suspects automated access (bots), it may run a short check in your browser and store the result in the cookie cf_clearance.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in a secure, working app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The bot check and its cookie are strictly necessary for this (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc., USA. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Storage: Cloudflare deletes this data once it is no longer needed for these purposes.

3. No tracking, no consent banner

The app itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. That is why there is no cookie banner.

If you switch the language (DE / EN), your browser remembers the choice in its local storage (entry lang, value de or en). It stays on your device and is never sent to me or anyone else. You can delete it in your browser settings. It is strictly necessary for the setting you asked for (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG).

4. The map

The map images, map fonts and icons are loaded by your browser directly from OpenFreeMap. In doing so, your browser sends your IP address, the map area requested, the page you came from, and your browser and operating system to OpenFreeMap. According to OpenFreeMap, its normal access logs contain no IP addresses and it uses no cookies; error logs may contain your IP address and are deleted after 7 days. During a security incident (for example an attack), it may log IP addresses for up to 30 days. Details: OpenFreeMap privacy policy.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in showing a map without running my own map server (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Recipient: Hyperknot Software Kft., Hungary (OpenFreeMap).

5. "Near me" (your location)

Using your location is optional. The app reads it only when you press Near me (in German: In der Nähe), once per press; it does not follow you. The first time, your browser asks whether the app may use your location; if you allowed it earlier, the browser may not ask again.

Your exact position is used only on your device to find the closest stops. It is not sent to me or to anyone else. However, two things can show roughly where you are:

  • The app loads the departures of the stops it found, so my server (Cloudflare, section 2) receives which stops you asked for, just as if you had tapped them on the map.
  • The map moves to your position, so your browser loads the map images for that area from OpenFreeMap (section 4).

Legal basis: your consent, given by pressing Near me and allowing location access (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time by removing the location permission for this site in your browser settings; this does not affect what happened before. Without it, you can still use the app by tapping stops on the map.

6. Live departures

Timetables and live departures come from Transitous. My server fetches them on your behalf, so Transitous never receives your IP address or any other data about you.

7. Email

If you email me, I receive your email address and your message. Cloudflare forwards emails sent to my address (see section 2) to my email account at Google (Gmail, Google Ireland Limited, Ireland). Google may transfer data to Google LLC, USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Purpose and legal basis: to answer your message, based on my legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Storage: I keep your message only as long as I need it to handle your request, or as long as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16), deleted (Art. 17) or restricted (Art. 18), and to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20). Just email wee@pangwayne.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one responsible for me, the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (poststelle@ldi.nrw.de).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
Where I process your data based on my legitimate interest (sections 2, 4 and 7), you can object at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation. I will then stop, unless I can show compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
